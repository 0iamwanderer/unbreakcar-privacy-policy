1. Overview

This Privacy Policy applies to Unbreakcar (“Unbreakcar”, “the Game”, “we”, “our”, or “us”). It describes the categories of information that may be processed when you install, access, or use the Game.

By using Unbreakcar, you acknowledge that information may be processed as described in this Policy and as permitted by applicable law.

2. Information We May Process

Device and technical information

Depending on the services enabled in the Game, we or our service providers may process technical information such as:

  • Device model, operating system, and app version
  • Device language and general region or country
  • App instance, installation, or device-related identifiers
  • Crash logs, diagnostics, performance, and stability information
  • Network and connection information required to provide online features

Gameplay and progression information

The Game may process information necessary to provide and maintain gameplay, including:

  • Player progress, race results, statistics, and achievements
  • Vehicles, upgrades, inventory, missions, rewards, and in-game currency
  • Game settings, preferences, and feature usage
  • Anti-cheat, abuse-prevention, or integrity-related events where applicable

Account information

If account, sign-in, cloud-save, or online profile features are provided, information such as a player ID, username, email address, authentication token, or account-linked progression may be processed as necessary to provide those features.

Support communications

If you contact us for support, we may process the information you choose to provide, such as your email address, message contents, screenshots, or technical details needed to investigate the issue.

3. How We May Use Information

Information may be used to:

  • Operate, maintain, and deliver Unbreakcar and its features
  • Save or synchronize player progress where supported
  • Improve gameplay, balancing, controls, graphics, performance, and stability
  • Diagnose bugs, crashes, errors, and compatibility issues
  • Process and verify purchases or restore purchased content
  • Protect the Game against fraud, cheating, abuse, and security threats
  • Provide customer support and respond to user requests
  • Measure feature usage and improve future releases
  • Comply with applicable legal obligations

We do not sell personal information in exchange for monetary payment.
